Privacy

Profit stores the financial entries you type locally in your browser using local storage. The current version does not require an account and does not send those entries to a server.

If advertising is enabled later, the advertising provider may process device, cookie, or consent information according to its own policies. Advertising will only be enabled after the required configuration and consent flow are added.

You can remove locally stored entries by deleting them in the app or clearing this site's browser data.

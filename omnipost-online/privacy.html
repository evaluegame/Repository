AiPost Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AiPost handles information when users upload videos, use AI-assisted post generation, connect social-media accounts, and publish content through official platform APIs.

1. Information AiPost processes

  • Uploaded media: videos and related metadata that you choose to upload.
  • Post content: titles, captions, descriptions, hashtags, scheduling choices, and destination selections.
  • Connected-account data: authorization tokens, account identifiers, basic profile information, and connection metadata returned by official platform APIs.
  • Technical data: information needed to operate the service, such as request timestamps, job status, upload size, and integrity hashes.

2. How information is used

AiPost uses information only to provide the features you request, including account connection, video analysis, AI-assisted metadata generation, upload integrity verification, publishing to selected platforms, connection testing, and publishing-status reporting.

3. TikTok data

If you connect TikTok, AiPost uses TikTok authorization and API data only to identify the authorized account and perform user-requested publishing functions. AiPost does not sell TikTok data or use TikTok user data for advertising.

4. AI processing

AiPost may perform video-frame analysis, speech transcription, and text generation locally in the user's browser or device where supported. Local AI processing is intended to reduce unnecessary transfer of analysis data to external AI providers. The original uploaded publishing file is handled separately from temporary AI analysis data.

5. Video storage and delivery

Uploaded videos may be stored temporarily on AiPost's hosting infrastructure so they can be delivered to the social platforms you select. AiPost is designed to preserve the original uploaded video bytes in its own publishing pipeline. Temporary files may be removed during service restarts, maintenance, cleanup, or redeployment.

6. Social-platform sharing

When you choose to publish, AiPost transmits the content and information required for the post to the selected destination platform through that platform's official API. Once the platform receives the content, its own privacy policy and terms apply.

7. Credentials and authorization tokens

AiPost does not ask for your social-media passwords. OAuth-based platforms authenticate you on their own authorization pages. Where connection data must be retained for publishing, AiPost stores protected connection information designed to prevent casual exposure. You should revoke platform access if you no longer want AiPost connected.

8. Data selling and advertising

AiPost does not sell personal information or connected social-account data. AiPost does not use connected-account data for targeted advertising.

9. Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain publishing history, satisfy security needs, or meet legal obligations. Temporary uploads may be deleted sooner because of hosting lifecycle events.

10. Your choices

You may stop using AiPost, disconnect or revoke connected social accounts through the relevant platform, and avoid uploading new content. Where supported, you may request deletion or correction of information associated with the service.

11. Security

AiPost uses reasonable technical safeguards, including HTTPS, protected authorization flows, encrypted connection data, and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Children and eligibility

AiPost is not intended to override the age or eligibility rules of connected platforms. Users must satisfy the applicable platform and legal requirements for the accounts they connect.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as AiPost features or platform requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information provided by the AiPost operator in the service or associated developer-account listing.
